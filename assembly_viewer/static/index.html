Protein Assembly Viewer

A tool for visualising how a protein complex assembles one subunit at a time. For each complex, the model greedily picks the next best subunit to join the growing structure, based on four scoring signals. You can step through the simulation manually, play it back automatically, or adjust the temperature to explore stochastic orderings.

What are the four scoring terms?

SASA (40%) Surface-Accessible Surface Area proxy. Approximated here by sequence length relative to total complex mass. Larger subunits tend to present more surface for interaction and are more likely to nucleate or join early. At step 0 (no context), this picks the largest subunit as the seed. ESM (35%) Sequence embedding similarity. Computed as mean cosine similarity between the candidate subunit's ESM-2 150M embedding and the embeddings of all already-assembled subunits. Subunits that are sequence-similar to the assembled core score higher — reflecting evolutionary co-adaptation. GO (15%) Gene Ontology coherence. Jaccard similarity between the candidate's GO molecular-function terms and those of the assembled subunits. Subunits that share function with the growing complex score higher. When FABLE is running, predicted GO terms (marked ✦) augment experimentally-annotated ones. PPI (10%) Protein–protein interaction confidence. Fetched from the STRING database (score 0–1). Subunits known to interact with already-assembled partners score higher. Falls back to 0.5 if STRING is unavailable.

Reading the circular graph

Node fill Dark tint: assembled (#1a2e1a), adding (#2e2a14), pending (#1c2128). Node stroke Unique color per UniProt identity — the same chain always has the same color regardless of step, making paralogs and homomers easy to distinguish. Node size Proportional to sequence length: r = clamp(10 + √seq_len × 0.55, 12, 28). Step badge Number inside assembled/adding nodes showing the 1-based assembly step at which that subunit joined. Edge opacity Forming bonds (dashed yellow) scale opacity and width with PPI score. Assembled edges use fixed opacity.

Ring complexes (GroEL, GroES, PCNA, TTR) show a ⬡ ring label in the centre.

What do the metrics mean?

Kendall's τ Rank correlation between the model's predicted assembly order and the experimentally-determined ground truth. τ = 1 means perfect order; τ = −1 means completely reversed. For ring complexes, τ is the maximum over all 2N cyclic rotations. τ sparkline Partial Kendall τ at each assembly step, computed over the assembled prefix. Shows how early the model locks in the correct relative ordering. Radar chart 4-axis score profile (SASA/ESM/PPI/GO) for the winner (blue filled) vs runner-up (red dashed). Useful for spotting which terms drove or contested the decision. Confidence bar Softmax probability of the chosen candidate at this step (temperature-calibrated). Delta shows margin over runner-up. Top-1 accuracy Fraction of steps where the model chose the same subunit as the ground-truth experimental order.

GO term pills

GO terms are colour-coded by namespace: F: Molecular Function, P: Biological Process, C: Cellular Component. Pills with a dashed border and ✦ marker are predicted by FABLE rather than sourced from UniProt experimental annotations.

FABLE predictions are only shown if the FABLE server is running locally on port 7860. If it's not running, only experimentally-annotated UniProt terms appear.

Rejected candidates table

At each step, every subunit that was not chosen is listed with its per-term scores and a mini-bar chart. This lets you see exactly why the model preferred one subunit over another.

Temperature slider

Normally the model greedily picks the top-scoring candidate. Raising the temperature > 1 softens the distribution, allowing lower-scoring subunits to occasionally be chosen. Click Rerun after adjusting.
